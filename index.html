JACKPOT BONUS

4+ MBP symbols award 15 free spins. In free spins, 3+ MBP symbols add 5 more spins.

4 / 5 / 6+ MBP symbols pay 3x / 5x / 100x the base bet.

On a winning tumble sequence, all orbs add together and multiply the symbol win. In free spins their values accumulate; the running total applies whenever a new orb lands with a symbol win. MBP payouts are added separately.

Double chance costs 25% more and increases natural free-spin chances. Turn it off to buy 15 free spins for 100x the base bet.

Slot rounds are capped at 5,000x the base bet, including all free spins and the triggering spin. Big, Superb, Mega and Sensational wins are celebrated at 20x, 35x, 50x and 100x.

Wingrid jackpots are separate play-credit prizes. Paid spins contribute 1% of their cost to four shared pools (0.4% Minor, 0.3% Major, 0.2% Mega, 0.1% Grand). Pools start at 25 / 100 / 500 / 2,500 credits and cap at 100 / 500 / 2,500 / 10,000.

A paid base or double-chance spin which does not start free spins can trigger a jackpot. Minimum cost: 0.20 credits. Trigger chance is 0.1% per credit of spin cost, capped at 2%, and after a jackpot the next one cannot trigger until you have played 150 more paid spins. Tier weights are 800 / 170 / 28 / 2. The awarded pool resets to its seed; choose gems to reveal three matching names. The prize is reserved for you until you finish, including after a reload.
